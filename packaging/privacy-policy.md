# Privacy Policy — Desktop Companion

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

Desktop Companion ("the app") is a desktop pet and focus-timer app.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data. It makes no network connections.

## What the app stores
Your preferences — chosen character, size, on-screen position, and timer
settings — are saved in a single file on your own computer:

    %APPDATA%\DesktopCompanion\settings.json

This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or deleting that file
removes it completely.

## Permissions
The app runs as a standard Windows desktop application (the Store lists this as
"uses all system resources", which is standard for packaged desktop apps). It
uses this only to draw its window on screen and, if you enable it, to add
itself to your Windows startup list. It does not read your files, camera,
microphone, or browsing activity.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.
