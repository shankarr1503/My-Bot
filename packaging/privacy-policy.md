# Privacy Policy — Desktop Companion

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

Desktop Companion ("the app") is a desktop pet and focus-timer app.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data, and it has no account, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

## Network use
The app works fully offline. It makes exactly one kind of network connection,
and only if you choose to use it: when you add your own photo as a pet with the
automatic background-removal feature, the app downloads a one-time image-cutout
model (~168 MB) from the public GitHub release it ships with, and caches it on
your device. Nothing about you, your image, or your usage is uploaded — the
download is a plain file fetch, and your photos are processed entirely on your
own computer. If you never use that feature, the app makes no network
connections at all.

## What the app stores
Your preferences — chosen character, size, on-screen position, and timer
settings — are saved in a single file on your own computer:

    %APPDATA%\DesktopCompanion\settings.json

This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or deleting that file
removes it completely.

## Permissions
The app runs as a standard Windows desktop application (the Store lists this as
"uses all system resources", which is standard for packaged desktop apps). It
uses this only to draw its window on screen and, if you enable it, to add
itself to your Windows startup list. It does not use your camera, microphone, or
browsing activity. It reads an image file only when you explicitly pick one to
use as your pet, and only that file.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.
